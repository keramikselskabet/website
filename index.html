About DAKS

The Danish Ceramic Society (Dansk Keramisk Selskab, DAKS) is a network of individuals, companies and research institutions working with ceramic materials and processes.

The society mainly deals with technical ceramics such as glasses, electroceramics, fuel cells, ceramic superconductors and catalysts.

DAKS organizes meetings and networking activities to strengthen collaboration between Danish industry and research.

The society is affiliated with the European Ceramic Society (ECerS) and the International Commission on Glass (ICG).

Membership

Membership is open to companies, universities, students and individuals with an interest in ceramic and glass materials.

DAKS PhD Prize

Every year the Danish Ceramic Society awards a PhD prize for outstanding doctoral work within ceramics and glass carried out at a Danish university.

The prize consists of a diploma and 2500 DKK.

Recent Winners

  • 2024 – Peter Grouleff Jensen
  • 2023 – Søren Strandskov Sørensen
  • 2022 – Esra Eray
