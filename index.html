About DAKS

The Danish Ceramic Society is a network for companies, universities and individuals with an interest in ceramic and glass materials.

The society promotes exchange of knowledge and contact between academia and industry within ceramic science, technology and applications.

DAKS is connected to the wider international ceramics and glass communities through the European Ceramic Society (ECerS) , International Ceramic Ferderation (ICF) and the International Commission on Glass (ICG) .

Membership

DAKS is open to students, researchers, companies and other organisations with an interest in ceramics and glass.

DAKS PHD PRIZE

Recognising excellent Danish PhD research

The DAKS PhD Prize is awarded annually by the Danish Ceramic Society to the best PhD thesis on ceramics or glass from a Danish university.

The prize consists of a diploma and 2,500 DKK.

Information about nomination will be available closer to the next deadline in January 2027.
